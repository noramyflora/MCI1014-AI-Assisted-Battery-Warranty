# Risk Register

## Project
AI-Assisted Battery Warranty Verification and Claim Management System for Bateriku.com

## Purpose
Identify potential risks affecting data quality, AI-assisted decisions, system reliability, and project delivery.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Incomplete customer warranty claim records | High | High | Validate mandatory fields and flag missing information. |
| R-02 | Incorrect AI-assisted warranty recommendations | Medium | High | Require human review before final claim decisions. |
| R-03 | Limited availability of historical claim data | High | Medium | Use clearly labelled synthetic data for prototype testing. |
| R-04 | Inconsistent data formats and duplicate records | Medium | Medium | Implement data cleaning and validation rules. |
| R-05 | Exposure of sensitive customer information | Medium | High | Apply data minimisation, access controls and anonymisation. |
| R-06 | Failure of automated validation or GitHub Actions | Medium | Medium | Run local tests and investigate CI validation failures. |
| R-07 | Incomplete documentation and traceability evidence | Medium | Medium | Maintain a requirements traceability matrix and evidence checklist. |

## Risk Monitoring
Risks should be reviewed throughout development. Likelihood and impact ratings are preliminary and must be reassessed using project evidence.

## Risk Ownership
Risk owners will be assigned according to the project responsibilities.
